# 👥 User Personas

### Persona 1: Amina (Daily Commuter / Muni University Student)
- **Age**: 22
- **Location**: Muni / Arua Hill
- **Need**: Safe, affordable, and predictable daily rides to campus and market without prolonged price negotiations.
- **Key Device**: Android smartphone, predominantly uses MTN data bundles and Mobile Money.

### Persona 2: Juma (Experienced Boda Rider / Driver)
- **Age**: 31
- **Stage**: Arua Main Market Stage
- **Motorcycle**: Bajaj Boxer 100 BM
- **Need**: Consistent trip requests throughout off-peak hours, higher daily net earnings, and safety protection against fare disputes.
- **Key Device**: Entry-level Android phone with limited battery and intermittent 3G connection.

### Persona 3: Bakole (Stage Chairman)
- **Role**: Head of Stage Operations
- **Need**: Oversight of registered riders on the stage, dispute resolution, and community credibility.
