# 🛵 Stage Operations & Ground Onboarding

The success of Pikipiki Arua depends on partnership with registered Boda Boda stages in Arua City.

## 1. Stage Hierarchy
1. **Stage Chairman**: Elected community leader representing 30–80 Boda riders at an assigned junction (e.g., Arua Main Market Stage, Onduparaka Stage).
2. **Stage Secretary / Marshal**: Verifies motorcycle condition, helmet availability, and records daily stage attendance.

## 2. Onboarding Workflow
1. **Field Agent Visit**: Team visits stage and briefs the Stage Chairman.
2. **Document Vetting**:
   - National ID Card (NIN verification)
   - Driving Permit (Class A)
   - Boda Logbook / Sales Agreement
   - 2 Referee contacts (one must be the Stage Chairman)
3. **App Training**:
   - Downloading `driver_app`
   - Using the incoming ride radar
   - Collecting Cash vs accepting MTN MoMo payments
   - Daily helmet and reflective jacket compliance
